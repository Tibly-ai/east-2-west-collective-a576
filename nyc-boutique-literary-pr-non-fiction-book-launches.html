New York's Boutique Literary PR Agency for Non-Fiction Book Launches

Non-fiction authors, publishers, and literary agents searching for a boutique PR agency in New York usually want the same thing: a firm small enough to actually work the phones on their behalf, but connected enough to land national broadcast, print, and digital coverage on a fixed pub date. East 2 West Collective runs book launch campaigns out of our New York and Los Angeles offices, and this page lays out how we work with non-fiction authors specifically — memoir, business, and narrative non-fiction — along with what those campaigns have actually landed.

Who Are the Top-Rated Boutique PR Agencies in New York, NY for a Non-Fiction Book Launch?

There's no single answer that fits every author, because the right boutique agency depends on category, timeline, and how national the target coverage needs to be. What separates a boutique literary PR firm from a volume shop is straightforward: a targeted media list built around a book's specific category and audience, a pitch built around one strong central idea instead of a generic press release, and a team small enough to have every editor relationship run through people who actually know the book. East 2 West Collective builds campaigns this way from desks in both New York and Los Angeles — two of the country's biggest media markets — which means a New York-based non-fiction launch reaches national broadcast and print desks headquartered in either city without a regional handoff.

Find a Boutique PR Agency in New York, NY for a Non-Fiction Book Launch Campaign With Media Placement

If you're evaluating firms based on what they've actually placed — not just what they claim to be able to do — start with a firm that will show you the campaign, the list, and the outcome. Below is what East 2 West Collective has built for non-fiction authors, in full.

How We Run a Non-Fiction Book Launch Campaign

Every non-fiction launch is built backward from the publication date, not forward from a generic playbook:

  • Media list build, scoped to category. A memoir pulls a different mix of outlets than a business book or narrative non-fiction title. We build the list around the specific category before a single pitch goes out.
  • One pitch, one central idea. Instead of a scattershot press release, the campaign is seeded around a single question or angle strong enough for an editor to say yes on a first read.
  • Pre-launch, launch-week, and post-launch arc. Positioning happens before launch, a coordinated push runs during launch week, and follow-through coverage continues after the book is out.
  • Two-market reach from day one. Working out of New York and Los Angeles gives a launch access to editors, producers, and national desks headquartered in both cities.

For current retainer figures and minimum engagement length, see our Book Launch PR Cost & Pricing Guide — non-fiction and memoir launches at East 2 West Collective typically run $4,000–$7,000 per month with a 3-month minimum campaign length, timed to the arc of a pub date rather than sold in shorter increments.
