What's Included in an Engagement

Our work, run from both offices, spans:

  • Media Relations — building and running the pitch to the outlets that matter for a specific talent, not a generic press list.
  • Brand Strategy — positioning a client's story so press coverage builds toward something bigger than a single placement.
  • Public Relations — the day-to-day: pitching, follow-up, media training coordination, and managing the story as it develops.
  • Strategic Partnerships — connecting talent to brand and influencer opportunities that reinforce the same story the press is telling.
  • Internal & Crisis Communications — protecting a reputation before a moment becomes a headline, and managing it if it already has.
  • Influencer Engagement & Experiential Marketing — extending a campaign beyond traditional press placements.

Every engagement starts with a media list build and positioning work before a single pitch goes out — the groundwork that determines whether outreach actually lands.
