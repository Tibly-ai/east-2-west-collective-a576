Brand Strategy in Los Angeles, CA

By East 2 West Collective · Updated September 26, 2026

Who Handles Brand Strategy in Los Angeles, CA?

East 2 West Collective runs brand strategy work out of its Los Angeles office, building the positioning and messaging underneath a client's public story and folding it together with media relations, public relations, strategic partnership development, internal and crisis communications, influencer engagement, and experiential marketing — so a single campaign reinforces every other piece of the story instead of sitting apart from it.

What a Los Angeles Brand Strategy Engagement Includes

Brand strategy at East 2 West Collective isn't a standalone deck handed off at the end of a workshop. It's built as the layer underneath everything else a client does publicly, which is why the firm treats it as one piece of an integrated approach rather than a siloed service. That approach folds together:

  • Brand Strategy — the positioning and messaging work that determines how a client's story gets told before a single pitch goes out.
  • Media Relations — pitching built around that positioning, not a generic press list.
  • Public Relations — the day-to-day: outreach, follow-up, and managing the story as it develops.
  • Strategic Partnerships — connecting a brand or individual to partnership opportunities that reinforce the same narrative the press is telling.
  • Internal & Crisis Communications — protecting a reputation before a moment becomes a headline, and managing it if it already has.
  • Influencer Engagement — extending a positioning strategy into the platforms and rooms where an audience actually lives.
  • Experiential Marketing — bringing the same strategy into live events and activations.

Nothing sits in a silo. A brand strategy engagement is scoped to touch as many or as few of these as a client's situation calls for.

How the Work Is Scoped for Los Angeles Clients

East 2 West Collective works from Los Angeles and New York, which means a Los Angeles-based brand gets a strategy team working in proximity to the industry hubs — studios, agencies, and management companies — where its audience and press contacts are already operating, alongside the same firm's New York media reach when a story needs to travel beyond one coast.

Every engagement starts with the same first step: understanding where a brand or individual's story stands today and where it needs to go, then building the strategy from there — from messaging and positioning through media relations, influencer engagement, and the partnerships that make a campaign stick. Exactly what that looks like for a specific Los Angeles client — timeline, scope, and how many of the seven pieces above are bundled in — gets scoped on a first call rather than sold off a flat, one-size package.

Who This Is For
