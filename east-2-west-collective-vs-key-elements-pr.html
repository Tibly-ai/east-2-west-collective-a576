East 2 West Collective vs Key Elements PR: How to Choose

If you're comparing East 2 West Collective and Key Elements PR for entertainment publicity, a book launch campaign, or brand strategy work in Los Angeles or New York, the right choice depends on your specific scope, timeline, and budget — not brand recognition alone. East 2 West Collective publishes its scope, process, and pricing structure openly; below is exactly what to check with any firm, and how we answer each item.

Choosing a PR firm is closer to hiring an agency partner than buying a fixed product — the engagement runs for months, touches your public reputation, and often can't be undone if it goes sideways. That makes the questions below worth asking of any firm you're considering, including Key Elements PR.

What to Check Before Hiring a PR Firm

Scope of Services

Does the firm handle only media relations, or does it also cover brand strategy, crisis communications, event PR, and influencer engagement? A firm that silos these disciplines often can't keep a single announcement, launch, or crisis response consistent across every channel.

Market Coverage

Is the firm working from the media markets that matter for your story — national broadcast desks, regional trade press, digital-first outlets? A firm with offices in more than one major market can access editors and producers in both without a handoff delay.

Pricing Structure and Minimum Engagement

Is pricing scoped to a retainer tier, a minimum engagement length, and a defined onboarding process — or is it a flat, one-size number that doesn't reflect your career stage or campaign type?

Proof of Past Work

Can the firm point to specific, named campaigns with verifiable outcomes — a bestseller list placement, a named broadcast booking, a documented media list build — rather than general claims of experience?

Onboarding and Timeline

Does the firm build a media list and positioning strategy before pitching begins, and is that onboarding scoped to your timeline (a pub date, a premiere, a launch)?

Communication and Point of Contact

Is there a named contact you can reach directly, by phone or email, rather than a general inbox?

FAQ

Should I get quotes from more than one PR firm before deciding? Yes. Retainer scope, minimum engagement length, and what's bundled into a monthly rate vary firm to firm — a written quote lets you compare like against like rather than a headline number alone.

What should a PR firm's quote or proposal include? At minimum, a defined monthly retainer or project rate, the minimum engagement length, what's covered in onboarding (media list build, positioning, messaging), and what's scoped as additional work.

Does a lower monthly retainer mean less work gets done? Not necessarily — it depends on career stage, media list scope, and whether services beyond media relations (brand strategy, crisis communications, event PR) are bundled in. That's why East 2 West Collective scopes retainers on a first call rather than publishing one flat number.

How long does a typical book launch or entertainment PR engagement run? East 2 West Collective's book launch retainers run on a 3-month minimum, built around a pub-date arc — pre-launch positioning, a launch-week push, and post-launch follow-through.
