How Do You Choose an Event PR Agency in Los Angeles?

By East 2 West Collective · Updated October 10, 2026

Choose an agency with hands-on experience running pre- and post-event press, celebrity guest coordination, and red carpet logistics for the specific type of event you're planning — galas, fashion shows, award shows, restaurant launches, or openings. East 2 West Collective runs event PR from a Los Angeles office and has coordinated publicity for galas, fashion shows, award shows, and openings across the city.

Event PR is not the same discipline as a single press release or a one-off interview booking. A real event campaign has to work on both sides of the date: building media and sponsor interest before anyone arrives, then making sure the right people — press, influencers, and talent — actually show up and get covered once the doors open. Here's what to check before you hire anyone to run that for you.

Experience With Your Exact Type of Event

Ask for a list of events the agency has actually worked, not a general capabilities pitch. A firm that has run fundraising galas, fashion shows, awards ceremonies, and retail openings will approach each differently — a casino opening needs different media and guest-list strategy than a philanthropy gala or a magazine launch party.

Pre-Event Sponsor and Media Outreach

A good event PR partner starts well before the event date, reaching out to potential corporate sponsors and building a media list that spans print, radio, TV, and online/social coverage. If an agency's pitch only describes "day-of" activity, that's a gap — the groundwork laid beforehand is often what determines whether press and sponsors show up at all.

Celebrity and Guest-List Coordination

Celebrity attendance is one of the strongest draws for media attention at any event. Ask whether the agency has an existing network of entertainment contacts it can tap to add event-appropriate names to a guest list, rather than starting from zero once you've signed a contract.

Day-Of Execution: Red Carpet and Media Logistics

On the day itself, someone needs to coordinate red carpet arrivals for both celebrity and media guests and set up interviews and photo opportunities with hosts and talent. Ask exactly who handles this on-site, how many people will be present, and how photo/interview logistics are structured so coverage keeps rolling after the event ends, not just during it.

Questions to Ask Before You Hire

  • Have you run an event in this exact category before — gala, fashion show, award show, or opening?
  • How do you approach sponsor outreach before the event date, and when does that work start?
  • What's your process for bringing celebrity or notable guests onto a client's guest list?
  • Who physically handles red carpet and media coordination the day of the event?
  • How do you keep coverage going after the event itself is over?
  • Can I see examples of past events you've coordinated publicity for?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose an event PR agency in Los Angeles?

Check for direct experience with your event category (gala, fashion show, awards show, or opening), a clear pre-event sponsor and media outreach process, an existing network for celebrity guest coordination, and a named team for day-of red carpet and media logistics. Ask to see a list of past events, not just a general pitch.

What does event PR actually include?

Based on East 2 West Collective's published process, it includes reaching out to potential corporate sponsors before the event, building a media list across print, radio, TV, and online/social coverage, tapping an entertainment contact network for guest-list additions, and coordinating red carpet arrivals plus interviews and photo set-ups during the event itself.

Does East 2 West Collective only work on entertainment events?

No. Its published event history spans galas and fundraisers, fashion and film events, award shows, book launch parties, television specials, and retail or community openings, including the Horseshoe Casino opening and Buca di Beppo restaurant openings.

How far in advance should I bring in an event PR team?

The agency's described process runs on both sides of the event date — sponsor and media outreach happens before the event, and coordination continues through the event itself. Reach out as early as possible once you have an event date and venue locked in.
