Retainer vs. Project-Based: Which Model Fits a New York Launch?

Entertainment publicity in New York generally runs one of two ways:

  • Monthly retainer — ongoing representation across a client's full career: press, philanthropic work, business endeavors, endorsement deals, and partnerships, not just a single moment.
  • Project-based engagement — a defined campaign built around one launch: a film release, a show premiere, or a single project, with a fixed scope and timeline.

East 2 West Collective builds a strategic blueprint for each client rather than working from a template, so the right model depends on whether the goal is a single launch moment or a persona built to last across a whole career.

What's Included in an Entertainment PR Engagement

Per East 2 West Collective's integrated approach, entertainment publicity work is never siloed — it folds together brand strategy, media relations, public relations, strategic partnership development, internal and crisis communications, influencer engagement, and experiential marketing, with each piece built to reinforce the next.

A standard entertainment engagement typically includes:

  • A brand persona built around the client's full career — not just the headline moment — covering philanthropic work, business endeavors, and endorsement deals and partnerships
  • A strategic blueprint developed specifically for the client and continuously refined as the campaign runs
  • Media relations and pitching to secure exposure that carries weight, not just column inches
  • Access to strategists who specialize in television networks, individual shows, and feature films for cross-media communications campaigns
  • Integration with brand strategy, influencer engagement, and experiential marketing where the campaign calls for it

FAQ

What is the average cost of retaining an entertainment PR firm in New York for a project launch? Project-based fees and ongoing monthly retainers are tailored specifically to the scope, media list size, campaign length, and whether crisis or influencer capabilities are included.

What's the difference between a retainer and a project-based fee for entertainment PR? A retainer covers ongoing representation across a client's full career — press, philanthropic work, business endeavors, and partnerships. A project-based fee covers a single defined launch, such as a film, show, or project, with a fixed scope and timeline.

Does East 2 West Collective work with talent, networks, and productions, or just individual clients? Depending on your needs, crisis support can be bundled into your main agreement or handled as a separate engagement.

How long does a typical New York entertainment or project launch campaign run? Campaign timelines are built around your specific release schedule, with retainers continuing on an ongoing basis.

Is there a setup or onboarding fee for a New York entertainment PR campaign? Setup and onboarding requirements are evaluated on a case-by-case basis during our initial consultation.
