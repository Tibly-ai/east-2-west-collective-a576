What Affects Your Price

Crisis management retainers vary because no two situations arrive the same way. A few factors that typically shape the fee for Los Angeles-based clients:

  • How fast the response needs to start. A retainer that's already in place before an incident functions differently — and is priced differently — than an emergency activation called in after a story has already broken.
  • How many audiences need to hear from the client at once. Crisis work often means coordinating an external media statement, an internal message to staff, and messaging to partners or sponsors simultaneously — through our Internal & Crisis Comms capability.
  • How long the situation runs. A single-cycle story requires a different level of engagement than a matter that plays out across [ENGAGEMENT_LENGTH_MONTHS] months of ongoing coverage.
  • Whether crisis work is layered onto existing brand strategy. Because East 2 West Collective builds crisis communications as one piece of an integrated approach with brand strategy, media relations, and strategic partnerships, clients already engaged with us across other services may see this reflected in the retainer structure.
