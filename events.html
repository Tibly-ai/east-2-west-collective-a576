PR Events / Los Angeles

Every Room
Needs A
Headline.

From red carpet arrivals to sponsor decks, East 2 West Collective runs the PR behind Los Angeles events, before the doors open and long after the last guest leaves.

How We
Work An
Event

E2W has tremendous experience handling the PR for Los Angeles based events. Our strategic outreach runs on both sides of the event date, hitting traditional sources including print, radio and TV, and going viral online through blog communities and social networking. It extends much further than a single press release.

Pre-event coordination includes reaching out to potential corporate sponsors. We know celebrity attendance is one of the strongest draws for media attention, so we dial into our network of entertainment contacts to get event appropriate names added to a client's guest list.

During the event itself, we coordinate red carpet arrivals for both celebrity and media guests and facilitate interviews and photo set-ups with media, hosts and talent, so the coverage keeps rolling once the night is over.

From restaurant launches to philanthropy fundraisers and award shows to casino openings, E2W has had a hand in coordinating all types of events.
