Short-Notice Literary PR: Rapid National Media Campaigns on a Tight Timeline

A publication date does not always wait for a publicist to be lined up months in advance. Whether a launch window shifted, a publisher's in-house team ran out of runway, or a book simply needs a stronger media push than it is currently getting, authors and publishers often require national media execution with weeks, not quarters, to work with.

East 2 West Collective runs author and book publicity campaigns out of our bicoastal offices in Los Angeles and New York. On a rapid-start timeline, our boutique team compresses core publicity workflows without skipping critical steps. Instead of staging campaign phases sequentially over several months, we launch them in parallel:

  • Targeted Media List Building: Built simultaneously across national broadcast networks, high-impact digital platforms, and major print outlets tailored to your book's category and subject matter.
  • Pitch Material Development: Developed prior to the campaign start date so media desks receive sharp, authoritative messaging the moment outreach begins.
  • Author Press & Photo Coordination: Facilitated alongside media pitching to ensure author imagery and interview assets are immediately available for press features.

By deploying small, experienced teams grounded in the nation's two major media capitals, we secure high-value placements that build lasting authority.

Proven Case Study: Rapid Execution and Unsolicited National Broadcast Press

When author Justine Bateman and Akashic Books selected East 2 West Collective to lead the public relations campaign for her book Face: One Square Foot of Skin (released April 6, 2021), we executed a highly focused media strategy centered on modern societal attitudes toward women and aging.

Our bicoastal team built a strategic media list spanning national broadcast programs, women’s-focused digital platforms, and high-end magazines. The campaign delivered immediate, high-impact results:

  • National Broadcast Openers: Kickstarted the launch on April 1, 2021, with a one-on-one interview between Justine Bateman and Hoda Kotb on The Today Show.
  • Targeted Multi-Guest Segments: Secured an April 2, 2021 feature on The Tamron Hall Show pairing Justine with Paulina Porizkova to expand the conversation surrounding women aging in front of the camera.
  • Top-Tier Print & Digital Features: Landed inclusion in Town & Country magazine's spring reading list.
  • Immediate Amazon Sell-Outs: The concentrated campaign strategy caused Amazon.com to sell out of the book twice within the first week of release.
  • Unsolicited Inbound Media Requests: Driven by the authority of the initial media push, major outlets including Time Magazine and 60 Minutes Australia requested interviews without requiring direct pitches.

As Justine Bateman noted: "Elizabeth Much at E2W Collective handled the launch of my book... Her concentrated approach caused Amazon.com to sell out of the book twice within the first week of the book's release, and caused outlets like Time Magazine and 60 Minutes Australia to knock on our door, unsolicited."
