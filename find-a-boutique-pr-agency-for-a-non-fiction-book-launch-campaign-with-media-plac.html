How East 2 West Collective Structures a Non-Fiction Launch Campaign

Non-fiction book publicity requires a vastly different strategic blueprint than fiction. A fiction campaign often focuses on plot synopses, whereas a non-fiction launch positions the author as a critical subject-matter expert on an issue national editors, bookers, and producers are covering right now. East 2 West Collective grounds every pitch in the author’s core expertise, connecting book topics directly to active news cycles and cultural debates.

1. Strategic Positioning & Campaign Blueprint

Instead of pitching "a new book release," our team identifies the single core question or cultural thesis behind your manuscript. We construct a narrative blueprint that frames your research, memoir, or industry analysis around timely public conversations, establishing why your perspective is essential reading today.

2. Category-Specific Media List Development

Mass press releases fail in top-tier markets. We curate tailored, highly targeted press lists across national broadcast television, women’s-focused digital outlets, business publications, and high-end print magazines in New York, Los Angeles, and national syndicate markets.

3. Timed Outreach & Fast-Turnaround Pitching

Outreach is timed strategically relative to your official publication date to build momentum, maximize pre-orders, drive first-week sales spikes, and establish a permanent media platform for the author that lasts long past launch week.
