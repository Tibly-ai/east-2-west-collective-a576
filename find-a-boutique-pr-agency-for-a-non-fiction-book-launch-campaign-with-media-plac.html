How East 2 West Collective Structures a Non-Fiction Launch Campaign

Non-fiction publicity requires a specialized strategic blueprint. Our agency follows a structured sequence designed to build sustained press cycles, maximize pre-orders, and establish a permanent media platform for the author:

  1. Strategic Positioning & Campaign Blueprint: We extract the core thesis behind your book. Instead of sending out standard announcements, we frame your expertise around timely news cycles, cultural debates, and national trends that editors and producers are covering right now.
  2. Category-Specific Media List Development: We build a curated press list tailored specifically to your book’s domain. Our outreach targets national broadcast television, business publications, women's-focused digital platforms, and high-end print magazines across Los Angeles, New York, and national syndicates.
  3. Timed Pitching & Media Outreach: Pitching is synchronized relative to your official publication date to maximize first-week sales spikes, drive retail demand, and secure sustained follow-up commentary slots.
  4. Broadcast & Media Preparation: Through specialized training, we prepare authors to walk into live broadcast television, radio, and podcast interviews confident and ready to deliver sharp, memorable soundbites.
  5. Event PR & Strategic Forums: For launch parties and virtual panels, we manage the PR lifecycle—from guest list curation and celebrity contacts to media coordination on the carpet—ensuring your event generates real press coverage.
