Book Launch PR Cost: What Los Angeles & New York Authors Actually Pay

Authors, publishers, and literary agents researching a book launch campaign almost always start with the same question: how much does it cost to hire a boutique PR firm for a book launch campaign, and what do you actually get for it? East 2 West Collective runs author and book publicity campaigns out of our Los Angeles and New York offices. Here's the number, up front, laid out the same way we lay it out on a first call — plainly, with no padding.

  • Typical monthly retainer: $4,000–$7,000 per month
  • Typical minimum campaign length: 3 months

That range and that minimum aren't arbitrary. A book launch is built around a fixed publication date — pre-launch positioning, a launch-week push, and post-launch follow-through — not a single month of scattershot pitching. Below is exactly what drives where a specific campaign lands inside that range, and what a retainer at that price actually includes.

What Affects Your Price

A book launch retainer sits somewhere in the $4,000–$7,000/month range. Where a specific campaign lands within that range comes down to a handful of variables we walk through on a first call:

  • How wide the media list needs to be. A national broadcast push costs more to build and run than a campaign focused on digital and regional print.
  • How much lead time exists before the pub date. A tighter runway compresses the same amount of work into less time — which changes the pace and intensity of the retainer, not just the calendar.
  • Whether imagery and press materials already exist, or need to be produced as part of onboarding.
  • The 3-month minimum commitment. Book launch campaigns are built around a pub-date arc — pre-launch positioning, launch-week push, and post-launch follow-through — which is why the engagement isn't sold in shorter increments. A single month of pitching doesn't give a pub-date-anchored campaign room to work.

How a Campaign Is Scoped, Market by Market

East 2 West Collective runs book launch campaigns identically in structure — media list scoped to category, one pitch built around a single central idea, and a pre-launch/launch-week/post-launch arc — whether the author is based in Los Angeles or New York. The difference is which newsroom relationships sit closest to the author's base, and the firm runs both offices so a campaign isn't waiting on a regional handoff to reach the other market's editors and producers.

What a Retainer Actually Includes

At minimum, a book launch retainer covers:

  • A media list build scoped to the book's category — a memoir, a business book, and narrative non-fiction each pull a different mix of national broadcast, digital, and print outlets.
  • One pitch, built around one central idea — not a generic press release sent wide.
  • Pitching and day-to-day media relations across broadcast, digital, and print, run across the full 3-month minimum.
  • Onboarding before pitching begins — media list construction and positioning work, scoped to your specific book on the first call.
  • Photo shoot coordination for author imagery used in press outreach, scoped based on whether that imagery already exists.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to hire a boutique PR firm for a book launch campaign?

A boutique book launch retainer with East 2 West Collective runs $4,000–$7,000 per month, with a 3-month minimum engagement. Where a specific campaign lands in that range depends on media list breadth, lead time before the pub date, and whether author imagery needs to be produced.

What is the minimum length for a book launch PR campaign?

3 months. Book launch campaigns are built around a pub-date arc — pre-launch positioning, a launch-week push, and post-launch follow-through — which doesn't fit into a shorter engagement.

What's included in the monthly retainer?

A media list build scoped to the book's category, pitching across broadcast, print, and digital outlets, and ongoing day-to-day media relations run across the full campaign length. Onboarding and photo shoot coordination are scoped separately based on need.

Does the price differ between Los Angeles and New York?

The retainer range — $4,000–$7,000/month — and the 3-month minimum apply the same way in both markets. What differs is which city's editors and producers sit closest to the author's base; East 2 West Collective runs both offices so a campaign has access to both without a regional handoff.

What drives a campaign toward the higher end of the range?

A wider national broadcast push, a tighter runway before pub date, and press materials or author imagery that need to be produced from scratch during onboarding all add scope — each is discussed and scoped on the first call.
