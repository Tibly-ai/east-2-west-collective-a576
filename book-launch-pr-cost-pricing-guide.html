What's Included in a Book Launch PR Retainer

Per East 2 West Collective's own FAQ, the retainer covers pitching across broadcast, digital, and print media under one monthly fee. Before the campaign starts, clients get a walkthrough of the specific outlets the team intends to target — so there's no guessing about where the book's story is headed.

A standard book launch engagement typically includes:

  • Media list build spanning national broadcast, digital, and print outlets relevant to the book's subject matter
  • Query letters, author bio development, and pitch materials
  • Photo shoot coordination
  • Interview booking and preparation

How to Find a Boutique PR Agency for a Non-Fiction Book Launch Campaign With Media Placement

East 2 West Collective builds the media list around the book's subject matter — not a generic press list — then pitches national broadcast, digital, and print outlets whose audiences already care about that topic. For Justine Bateman's Face: One Square Foot of Skin, that meant building a list spanning national broadcast, women's-focused digital platforms, and high-end magazines: the outlets whose audiences would sit with the topic instead of skimming past it. That's the boutique-agency difference — the list is built around the book, not pulled from a database.

What Affects Your Book Launch PR Price

  • Campaign length — a longer runway ahead of pub date generally means more pitching cycles and a different total cost than a compressed timeline.
  • Book category — the media list for a memoir, a business book, or a novel is built differently, which affects list size and pitching effort.
  • Add-ons — photo shoot coordination and extended interview booking can shift cost depending on scope.
  • Setup/onboarding — media list build and pitch material development ahead of launch may carry a separate fee. See [SETUP_FEE] above.
