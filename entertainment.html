Entertainment PR

ENTERTAINMENT

The TV and film landscape keeps shifting under new digital services and a media environment that never sits still. We build entertainment publicity strategies that help talent, both established and emerging, cut through it.

Brand personas built for the whole career, not just the headline.

With a longstanding presence in the industry, we create entertainment publicity strategies for clients across the full spectrum, from names already known to careers still being built. Our approach maps out a brand persona that covers the client's career, philanthropic work, business endeavors, endorsement deals and partnerships.

These are not one size fits all campaigns. Every client gets a strategic blueprint that our team develops and continuously refines to secure media exposure that actually means something, not just column inches.

Our team also includes strategists who specialize in television networks and properties, individual shows and feature films. We work directly with those clients to build integrated cross-media communications campaigns that grow viewership and strengthen market position.
