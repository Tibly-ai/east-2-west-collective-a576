Project-Based & Short-Term Entertainment PR in Los Angeles

Not every artist, actor, or entertainment brand needs a 12-month retainer. Sometimes what you need is a publicist for one moment — a premiere, a single, a launch week, a crisis that needs handling now and doesn't need handling in June. East 2 West Collective runs entertainment publicity, brand strategy, and event PR out of our Los Angeles and New York offices, and we structure campaigns around what the project actually requires, not around a default 12-month contract.

This page is for anyone who's typed some version of "how do I hire an entertainment publicist in Los Angeles for a quick project" and wants a straight answer about how that actually works with us.

What "Project-Based" Means at East 2 West Collective

A project-based engagement is scoped around a defined event, release, or window of time — rather than an open-ended monthly retainer. That could mean:

  • A single release, premiere, or public appearance
  • A short media push tied to a specific date
  • A crisis response engagement scoped to the active situation
  • An event PR campaign built around one date on the calendar

How a Short-Term Campaign Is Built

Regardless of campaign length, our process follows the same core structure we use across entertainment, author, and brand work:

Media list build. We build a targeted list matched to the project — not a generic blast list — spanning the outlets whose audience actually overlaps with the story.

Positioning and the pitch. We identify the single question or angle the story should be built around, then seed the pitch to the outlets most likely to sit with it rather than skim past it.

Coordinated timing. For project-based work, timing is the whole game — pitching has to land in the window that matters (a premiere date, a release date, a single news cycle) rather than unfold over months.

Who This Is For

This structure fits actors, musicians, creators, and entertainment brands who have one specific moment to cover — a premiere, an event, a release, or a reputational issue that needs handling now — and don't need or want an ongoing monthly retainer. FAQ

How do I hire an entertainment publicist in Los Angeles for a quick project? Start with a first call. We scope the project — the date, the story, the outlets that matter — and build a media list and pitch plan around that window rather than an open-ended retainer.
