Who Runs Author PR Campaigns in Los Angeles and New York?

By East 2 West Collective · Updated November 26, 2026

East 2 West Collective runs author PR campaigns out of its Los Angeles and New York offices, building a media list, pitch strategy, and pub-date timeline for each book from scratch. The agency has landed authors on the Today Show, Tamron Hall, Good Morning America's third hour, and the New York Times bestseller list, and placed titles with Time Magazine and 60 Minutes Australia.

A book launch competes with everything else on an author's plate — the writing is done, but getting the right outlets to notice is its own full-time job. East 2 West Collective's author work sits inside its broader brand marketing and consultancy practice, where media relations, public relations, and strategic positioning are built to reinforce each other rather than operate as one-off tactics.

What an Author PR Campaign Includes

East 2 West Collective's author work draws from its eleven PR and marketing disciplines, applied specifically to a book's publication date. For an author launch, that typically means:

  • Media list development built around the book's actual audience — national broadcast, women's-focused or category-specific digital platforms, and high-end print, rather than a generic mass list.
  • Pitch development seeded around a single strong angle or question the book raises, not a summary of the jacket copy.
  • Broadcast and talk show pitching, including producers at national morning shows and daytime programs.
  • Press release development and distribution, written and sent to the right desks.
  • Media training, preparing the author to walk into any interview — TV, radio, or print — ready and confident.
  • Virtual or live event coordination, giving a book's central conversation a venue beyond a single interview segment.
  • Crisis management, on standby if a book's subject matter or an author's history draws unexpected attention.

How the Process Works

East 2 West Collective starts every author campaign by interviewing the author (and anyone else involved, such as a publisher or co-author) to build a blueprint of target outlets before any pitching begins. From there:

  1. Writing comes first — query letters, author bios, and photo shoot coordination are built out before outreach starts.
  2. Pitching follows the blueprint — outreach is timed and targeted, not blasted.
  3. The timeline is anchored to the pub date — planning works backward from the publication date, the same fixed point every other deadline is built around.
  4. First hits typically land in four to six weeks. Reaching the right editor or producer, and getting a real response, takes that long as a rule — there's no shortcut around it.

What to Look For in an Author PR Firm

Not every PR firm pitches books well, and East 2 West Collective's own FAQ lays out what to ask before signing with anyone:

  • Ask to see work with authors or books in your category, not just entertainment clients generally.
  • Ask who specifically will be pitching your story day to day.
  • Ask how the firm will keep you posted on what's landing and what isn't.
  • Ask what's covered in the retainer — East 2 West Collective's retainer covers pitching across broadcast, digital, and print media under one monthly fee, with the specific target outlets walked through before the campaign starts.

A firm that welcomes those questions directly, rather than deflecting them, is the one worth hiring.
