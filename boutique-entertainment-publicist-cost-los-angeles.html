What Affects Your Price

Where the talent is in their career. A retainer for a new or emerging name is scoped differently than one for an established client, because the media list, the pitch angle, and the amount of positioning work up front all differ. [NEW_TALENT_SCOPE_DETAIL — describe what changes in scope for emerging vs. established talent].

How much groundwork exists already. Clients arriving with little to no press history, a bio, or professional photography typically need more onboarding work — positioning, messaging, and asset development — before outreach starts. That shows up in the setup fee, not the monthly retainer.

Campaign length and intensity. A short activation around a single release, single, or project moment is priced differently than an ongoing monthly retainer built to develop a career narrative over time.
