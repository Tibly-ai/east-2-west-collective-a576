New York Book Launch PR: Campaigns Built to Land on the Bestseller List

Authors and publishers in New York don't need another firm that promises "buzz." They need a team that has actually put a book on the New York Times bestseller list and can explain, in plain terms, how it happened. East 2 West Collective runs author and book publicity campaigns out of offices in Los Angeles and New York, and this page walks through what a New York book launch campaign with our team looks like, from media list to pub date.

Book Launch PR Built for New York's Media Market

New York is home to the outlets — national broadcast, major dailies, glossy print, and the digital publications that set the conversation — that can turn a book release into a real cultural moment instead of a quiet drop. Landing coverage in that market takes a media list built specifically around a book's subject matter, pitches timed to the publication date, and a team that knows which editors and producers actually cover books versus which ones will let a pitch sit unread.

What a New York Bestseller Campaign Includes

A standard East 2 West Collective book launch engagement covers pitching across broadcast, digital, and print media under one monthly retainer. Before the campaign starts, clients get a walkthrough of the specific outlets the team intends to target, so there's no guessing about where the book's story is headed. That engagement typically includes:

  • Media list build spanning national broadcast, digital, and print outlets relevant to the book's subject matter
  • Query letters, author bio development, and pitch materials
  • Photo shoot coordination
  • Interview booking and press day scheduling

For current retainer figures and campaign length, see our Book Launch PR Cost & Pricing Guide.

Best Book Launch PR Agencies in New York to Get on Bestseller Lists

Authors searching for the best book launch PR agencies in New York to get on bestseller lists should look for a firm with a documented bestseller campaign, not just a client roster. East 2 West Collective's campaign for Michael Richards' memoir, Entrances & Exits, landed the book on the New York Times bestseller list — built on a media strategy timed around the book's publication date and outlets chosen for where the story would actually land, not just where a pitch could be sent.

Proof: Campaigns That Reached the Bestseller List and Beyond

Michael Richards, Entrances & Exits — East 2 West Collective ran the publicity campaign for the Emmy-winning actor's memoir, timed around his continued popularity from Seinfeld's ongoing life on Netflix. The campaign landed the book on the New York Times bestseller list.

Justine Bateman, Face: One Square Foot of Skin (Akashic Books) — The campaign built a media list spanning national broadcast, women's-focused digital platforms, and high-end magazines around a single question: why can't women feel free to age on their own terms? In Bateman's own words: "Her concentrated approach caused Amazon.com to sell out of the book twice within the first week of the book's release, and caused outlets like Time Magazine and 60 Minutes Australia to knock on our door, unsolicited."
