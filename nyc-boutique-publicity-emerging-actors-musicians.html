Entertainment Publicists in NYC for Upcoming Actors and Musicians

New York is not short on publicists. It's short on publicists who will actually pick up the phone for a client who isn't famous yet. East 2 West Collective runs entertainment publicity out of offices in both New York, NY and Los Angeles, CA, and this page is for the rising actor, musician, or creator asking the question we hear most on a first call: who takes on talent at this stage of a career, and what do they actually do for you?

Below is our answer — in the same plain terms we use in that first conversation.

Who This Is For

This page is built for New York-based (or New York-bound) actors and musicians who are past the "no press" stage but not yet a household name — the client who needs a real media strategy, not a mass blast to a generic press list. If that's where you are, this is the work we do.

What a Boutique NYC Publicity Engagement Covers

Custom Media Strategy No two rising talents have the same story, so no two media lists look the same. We build a strategy around what's actually pitchable right now — a project, a moment, a body of work — rather than pitching "the person" in the abstract.

Bio & Positioning Development Before a single pitch goes out, we sharpen how a client is introduced to media: the bio, the angle, the one or two things a producer or editor needs to know in the first ten seconds. This is the same positioning work we do for authors and brand clients across our roster — the discipline doesn't change with the industry.

Interview Booking & Media Relations Ongoing outreach and relationship management with the outlets that actually cover emerging talent — not just the outlets with the biggest name. Media relations, public relations, and strategic partnership development all sit under one plan instead of three separate vendors.

Crisis & Reputation Readiness Emerging talent gets discovered by media faster than most expect. Having a crisis communications plan in place before it's needed — not after — is part of how we build long-term publicity relationships, not one-off placements.

Why New York Specifically

Our New York office works alongside our Los Angeles office rather than as a satellite of it. For talent based in New York — or splitting time between the coasts — that means a single point of contact who understands both the New York media landscape and the entertainment-industry trade press that tends to run out of Los Angeles.
