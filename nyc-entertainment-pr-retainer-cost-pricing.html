What Affects Your Price

A New York entertainment PR retainer is not one-size-fits-all. The final number on a proposal reflects:

  • Scope of the launch. A single-market press push costs differently than a campaign spanning national broadcast, entertainment trades, and digital/influencer outreach.
  • Lead time before the launch date. Shorter runways often require compressed media list-building and pitch development, which affects setup costs.
  • Talent or brand visibility. An established name with existing media relationships requires different positioning work than a debut launch introducing someone new to entertainment press.
  • Integration with other services. East 2 West Collective's entertainment publicity work often sits alongside brand strategy, media relations, and crisis/internal communications — bundling these can affect the total monthly figure.
