Brand Strategy Mistakes to Avoid

By East 2 West Collective · Updated October 11, 2026

7 Brand Strategy Mistakes to Avoid in Los Angeles

The most common brand strategy mistake in Los Angeles is treating brand strategy as a single deliverable — a press release, a logo refresh, a one-off media hit — instead of an integrated plan that ties messaging, media relations, partnerships, and crisis readiness together. East 2 West Collective builds that integrated plan for every client, because a brand story that only lives in one channel stops working the moment that channel goes quiet.

Below are the mistakes we see most often from brands and individuals building a strategy in a competitive, fast-moving market like Los Angeles — some on the buyer's side, some on the agency's side — and what to do instead of each one.

Mistake 1: Choosing a PR Partner on Price Alone

Brand strategy gets undervalued when it's shopped like a commodity. The cheapest proposal is rarely the one built around your specific audience, timeline, and goals — it's often a template with your name swapped in. A strategy that isn't tailored to the client in front of it tends to produce generic coverage that doesn't move the needle, no matter how many placements land.

What to do instead: Ask what the strategy actually includes before asking what it costs. A real blueprint should name the specific media, partnerships, and messaging approach built for your brand — not a reused plan.

Mistake 2: Skipping the Written Blueprint

Verbal agreements and loose "we'll figure it out as we go" plans are a mistake on both sides of the table. Without a written strategic blueprint, there's no way to measure whether a campaign is on track, and no shared understanding of what "done" looks like.

What to do instead: Work with a partner who builds a tailored blueprint from a defined set of disciplines, rather than improvising discipline by discipline. East 2 West Collective builds a blueprint from eleven PR and marketing disciplines, tailored to the client, not a one-size-fits-all package.

Mistake 3: Letting Each Discipline Work in a Silo

Media relations without brand strategy behind it produces scattered coverage. Influencer engagement without a crisis plan leaves a brand exposed the moment something goes sideways. Treating these as separate vendors or separate workstreams is one of the most expensive mistakes a brand can make, because nothing reinforces anything else.

What to do instead: Insist that brand strategy, media relations, public relations, strategic partnerships, internal and crisis communications, influencer engagement, and experiential marketing are built to work together, with every piece reinforcing the next.

Mistake 4: Waiting Until a Crisis Hits to Build a Response Plan

Crisis management is the discipline most often skipped until it's urgently needed. By then, there's no time to build a thoughtful response — only a reactive one. A brand strategy that doesn't account for how the team responds when "a client's story turns unexpected" is an incomplete strategy.

What to do instead: Build crisis communications into the strategy from day one, as one of the standing disciplines in the blueprint, not an afterthought added after the first bad headline.

Mistake 5: Building a Persona Around One Moment, Not the Whole Career

A brand strategy that only accounts for the current headline — the current book, the current show, the current launch — misses the bigger opportunity. East 2 West Collective's entertainment publicity work maps a brand persona across a client's full career, philanthropic work, business endeavors, endorsement deals, and partnerships, because a persona built for one moment stops working the moment that moment passes.

What to do instead: Ask your strategist to account for where the brand is headed, not just what's launching this quarter.

Mistake 6: Overlooking Reach Beyond Los Angeles

A Los Angeles-based brand strategy that only accounts for the Los Angeles media market leaves coverage on the table. National broadcast, print, and digital outlets are headquartered on both coasts, and a bi-coastal presence matters when a story needs to reach producers and editors in both markets.

What to do instead: Work with a team with an actual footprint in both markets. East 2 West Collective operates bi-coastal offices in Los Angeles, at 1800 Century Park East #60, Los Angeles CA 90067, and in New York, at 745 Fifth Avenue, Suite 500, New York NY 10151 — giving Los Angeles clients a direct line into the New York media market as part of the same strategy, not a separate engagement.

Mistake 7: Not Confirming How Coverage Will Be Proven

Some campaigns chase volume — a long list of placements that don't actually build credibility. A brand strategy mistake worth avoiding is signing on without asking how results get tracked and reported.

What to do instead: Ask for a working record of real placements tied to real client names, not a promise of "exposure." East 2 West Collective maintains a running media coverage log of client segments, interviews, and features across broadcast, print, and digital — the kind of record that shows what a campaign actually delivers.

Key Takeaways

  • Choosing a PR partner on price alone usually buys a template, not a strategy.
  • A brand strategy without a written blueprint has no way to be measured.
  • Media relations, crisis comms, partnerships, and influencer engagement should reinforce each other, not operate separately.
  • Crisis communications belongs in the strategy from the start, not after the first problem.
  • A brand persona built for a whole career outlasts one built around a single headline.
  • A bi-coastal footprint matters for Los Angeles brands that need New York media reach too.
  • Ask for a real, working record of placements — not a promise of generic "exposure."

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it a mistake to hire separate vendors for PR, crisis comms, and influencer work?

It can be. When these disciplines aren't built to work together, a brand's messaging can end up inconsistent across channels. What if my brand doesn't have a crisis plan yet?

That's common, but it's worth fixing before a crisis, not during one. Crisis management is one of the standing disciplines in East 2 West Collective's blueprint and can be built into a brand strategy from the outset.
