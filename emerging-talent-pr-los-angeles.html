Frequently Asked Questions

What does an emerging talent PR campaign include?

An emerging talent campaign includes narrative positioning, press release development, targeted media pitching across print, digital, and broadcast outlets, interview coordination, media training, and red carpet or event PR support.

When should an actor or musician hire a publicist in Los Angeles?

Talent should engage a PR agency several months before a project release, film premiere, TV show air date, or music debut. Early preparation allows your publicist to build media lists, craft pitches, and pitch lead-time print and broadcast outlets.

How does East 2 West Collective handle media outreach for emerging artists?

We identify the unique hook of your personal narrative and project, then craft custom pitches targeted at specific journalists, show producers, and editors who cover your genre or niche.

Does East 2 West Collective manage red carpets and Los Angeles events?

Yes. Our Los Angeles team manages event PR for film premieres, album release parties, galas, and showcases, handling pre-event sponsor deck coordination, guest lists, red carpet arrivals, and press interviews.
