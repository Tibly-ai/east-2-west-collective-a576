Which Industries Does East 2 West Collective Represent?

By East 2 West Collective · Updated November 27, 2026

East 2 West Collective's client work falls into five core categories: TV & film talent, authors & literary clients, health & wellness brands, corporate & retail brands, and special events & galas. The bicoastal team, based in Los Angeles and New York, has built campaigns in each category that have landed national broadcast hits and, in at least one case, a New York Times bestseller.

TV & Film Talent

This is the agency's founding lane. East 2 West builds entertainment publicity strategies for established names and careers still being built, mapping a brand persona across career, philanthropic work, business endeavors, endorsement deals and partnerships rather than chasing a single news cycle. The team also includes strategists who work directly with television networks, individual shows and feature films on integrated cross-media campaigns. Clients and placements in this category documented on the Spotlight media coverage page include Loni Love (Tamron Hall Show, Good Day LA, Yahoo! Life), David Duchovny (Yahoo Entertainment, The View), Ernie Hudson (People, The Hollywood Reporter, TV Guide), Lori Loughlin (Us Weekly, First for Women), Niecy Nash (The Late Late Show), Andrew Yang (Good Morning America), Martin Kove (Access Hollywood) and Craig Melvin (The 700 Club, The Doctors). The team's full approach to this category is detailed on the Entertainment PR page.

Authors & Literary

Author campaigns extend beyond a single book launch into an ongoing platform, pairing brand strategy with the media relationships that get a pub date noticed. Justine Bateman's book Face: One Square Foot of Skin is the agency's flagship author case: a targeted media list and pitch built around one question led to a Today Show interview, a Tamron Hall Show segment, a Town & Country reading-list placement, two Amazon sell-outs in the book's first week, and unsolicited coverage from Time Magazine and 60 Minutes Australia. The full breakdown is on the Justine Bateman case study. Michael Richards' memoir Entrances & Exits is the agency's other documented author campaign, landing on the New York Times bestseller list. Other literary clients named across the site's Spotlight log include Adam Mansbach (The Rich Eisen Show) and Leslie Liautaud (The New York Times, Writer's Digest).

Health & Wellness

The wellness roster listed on the Brand Marketing & Consultancy page spans fitness platforms, studios and individual experts: YAS, CrossTrain LA, Jane Fonda, Power Yoga, Gurmukh, Richard Simmons, Mari Winsor, ExerciseTV, Kathy Kaehler, Dr. Colin Campbell, Ashley Koff R.D., Whole Foods Market, Yogi Tea, Core Body Reformer®, Empower Fitness, Power Plate and Pivotal 5. For this category, the agency folds brand strategy, media relations and influencer engagement together rather than running them as separate workstreams.

Corporate & Retail Brands

Corporate and retail work overlaps with the events side of the business: Whole Foods Market's Earth Day event and Horseshoe Casino's opening are both documented on the Events page, alongside restaurant openings including Buca di Beppo and Erewhon's 30th Anniversary Gala. Science-education company Generation Genius Inc. and reporting on the Los Angeles Business Journal also appear in the agency's media coverage log.

Events & Galas

East 2 West runs PR before, during and after an event date — pre-event sponsor outreach, celebrity guest-list placement, red carpet coordination, and media/photo set-ups once doors open. Documented galas and fundraisers include the Casting Society of America's Artios Awards, Save A Child's Heart West Coast Gala, The Dream Foundation fundraisers, the Physicians Committee for Responsible Medicine (PCRM) Gala, the Lili Claire Foundation, Much Love Animal Rescue events, the Humane Society of the United States Prop 2 Dinner, and A Rare Breed of Love in New York. Fashion, film and awards events on the same page include Fashion's LA Platform, the LA Fashion Awards, the Moss Adams Fashion Innovator Awards, and the Inside Weddings bridal fashion show and magazine launch.

Key Takeaways

  • East 2 West Collective's documented client work spans five categories: TV & film talent, authors & literary, health & wellness, corporate & retail, and events & galas.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does East 2 West Collective only work with celebrities?

No. While TV and film talent is a core category, the agency also represents authors, wellness and fitness brands, corporate and retail clients, and nonprofit galas and fundraisers, as shown across its Brand Marketing and Events client lists.

Can East 2 West Collective handle an author's book launch specifically?

Yes. The agency's documented approach builds a media list around the book's central theme, pitches a focused story angle, and identifies a forum for public conversation, as detailed in the Justine Bateman case study, which resulted in a Today Show interview, two Amazon sell-outs, and unsolicited coverage from Time Magazine.

Does the agency work with wellness and fitness brands outside of entertainment?

Yes. The wellness and fitness roster on the Brand Marketing & Consultancy page includes studios, platforms, and individual experts such as Jane Fonda, Richard Simmons, Whole Foods Market and Yogi Tea, handled through combined brand strategy, media relations and influencer engagement.

What kind of events has East 2 West Collective supported?

Documented events span galas and fundraisers, fashion shows and awards, and retail or restaurant openings, including the Casting Society of America's Artios Awards, the LA Fashion Awards, and the Horseshoe Casino Opening, all listed on the Events page.

Is there one firm that handles both entertainment talent and corporate brand PR?

Yes — East 2 West Collective runs both under one bicoastal team, pairing entertainment publicity strategists with brand strategy and media relations specialists so a client's story is handled consistently whether the audience is entertainment press or a retail/corporate trade outlet.
