Boutique Literary PR Services for Fast-Turnaround National Media Campaigns

Boutique Literary PR for Authors Who Need National Coverage — Fast

Authors, publishers, and literary agents searching for a boutique literary PR agency that can secure national media coverage on short notice are usually working against a fixed date: a pub date, a tour stop, a narrow press window that won't move. East 2 West Collective runs book launch campaigns out of our Los Angeles and New York offices, and our model is built for exactly that pressure — a tight runway, a real story, and a media list that has to land on the first pitch, not the fifth.

We're not a volume shop working a mass press list. We build a targeted media list scoped to a book's category and audience, write a pitch around one strong central idea, and put it in front of the broadcast, print, and digital editors who actually cover that subject — from our desks in two of the country's biggest media markets.

How We Move Fast on a Fixed Timeline

Every book launch campaign starts the same way, whether the runway is six months or six weeks:

  • Media list build, scoped to category. A memoir, a business book, or narrative non-fiction each pulls a different mix of national broadcast, digital, and print outlets. We build the list around the specific category before a single pitch goes out.
  • One pitch, one central idea. Instead of a scattershot press release, we seed the campaign around a single question or angle strong enough for an editor to say yes on a first read.
  • Pre-launch, launch-week, and post-launch arc. Because publicity for a book is anchored to a pub date, our campaigns are built backward from that date — positioning before launch, a coordinated push during launch week, and follow-through after.
  • Two-market reach from day one. Working out of Los Angeles and New York gives a launch access to editors, producers, and national desks headquartered in both cities without waiting on a regional handoff.

Proof: What Fast, Targeted Pitching Has Landed

Justine Bateman, Face: One Square Foot of Skin (with Akashic Books) — We built a media list spanning national broadcast, women's-focused digital platforms, and high-end magazines, seeded around a single central question about women, aging, and the pressure to look a certain way on camera. The results:

  • Today Show — a one-on-one interview between Justine Bateman and Hoda Kotb, kicking off the campaign on April 1, 2021.
  • Tamron Hall Show — Paulina Porizkova joined Justine on April 2, 2021 to widen the conversation.
  • Town & Country — listed Face on their spring reading list.
  • Unsolicited national coverage — Time Magazine and 60 Minutes Australia came to the story on their own, without a pitch sent their way.
  • Amazon.com sold out of the book twice within the first week of release.

Michael Richards, Entrances & Exits — East 2 West Collective ran the full publicity campaign behind the Emmy-winning actor's memoir, building a national media list spanning broadcast, print, and digital outlets and landing the book on the New York Times bestseller list.

FAQ

Are there boutique literary PR agencies that can secure national media coverage on short notice? Yes — East 2 West Collective runs book launch campaigns out of Los Angeles and New York built specifically around fixed publication dates, with a media list and a central pitch angle developed before the campaign starts so outreach can move as soon as the runway allows.

What kind of national coverage has East 2 West Collective actually landed for a book launch? Campaigns have landed placements including the Today Show, the Tamron Hall Show, and Town & Country, along with unsolicited coverage from Time Magazine and 60 Minutes Australia, and a New York Times bestseller listing for Michael Richards' memoir Entrances & Exits.

How fast can a media list be built before a launch? Every book launch retainer starts with a media list build and pitch material development scoped to the book's category — the exact timeline depends on lead time before pub date and is discussed and scoped on your first call.

Do you work with fiction and non-fiction authors, or just memoirs? Our proof points to date are memoir and narrative non-fiction campaigns (Justine Bateman, Michael Richards); the media list approach — built around category and audience — applies to other book categories as well, discussed on a first call.

Does East 2 West Collective handle author imagery and press materials, or just pitching? Photo shoot coordination for author imagery used in press outreach is scoped based on need and discussed on your first call, alongside the core media list build and pitching.

What markets does East 2 West Collective pitch to for a book launch? The team works out of Los Angeles and New York offices, pitching to editors, producers, and national desks in both markets as well as outlets specific to a book's subject matter.
