Choosing a publicist or PR firm is a bigger decision than picking a vendor off a rate card — the firm you hire will represent your name, your book, or your brand's reputation in front of the press. Below is what to check before signing with any firm, followed by how East 2 West Collective answers each item, using only what's published about our work.

What to Check Before Hiring a PR Firm

Specialization Fit

Not every PR firm works the same lane. A firm built for entertainment publicity and author campaigns pitches differently than a firm built for corporate communications or product launches. Ask any firm you're considering to name the category of work they run most often, and ask to see proof in that exact category.

East 2 West Collective: Our work is scoped to entertainment publicity, book launch campaigns, crisis management, brand strategy, and event PR — run out of our Los Angeles and New York offices for talent, authors, and brands.

Markets Covered

A firm's physical presence in your media market matters — access to local editors, producers, and outlets headquartered in that city isn't the same as pitching remotely from another region.

East 2 West Collective: We operate from two of the country's largest media markets, Los Angeles and New York, giving campaigns access to editors and producers headquartered in both cities.

Proof of Results

Case studies and named placements tell you more than a client list. Ask for specific campaigns, specific outlets, and specific outcomes — not general claims of "media relationships."

East 2 West Collective: Our published case studies include Michael Richards' memoir Entrances & Exits, which reached the New York Times bestseller list, and Justine Bateman's Face: One Square Foot of Skin, which landed a one-on-one Today Show interview with Hoda Kotb, a Tamron Hall Show segment, a Town & Country spring reading list mention, and unsolicited coverage from Time Magazine and 60 Minutes Australia. Amazon sold out of Face twice in its first week of release.

Pricing Transparency & Written Scope

A firm should be able to explain a price range, a minimum engagement length, and what's included before you sign — not just a single flat number with no context.

East 2 West Collective: For book launch campaigns, our published range is $4,000–$7,000 per month with a 3-month minimum engagement, built around a publication date arc: pre-launch positioning, a launch-week push, and post-launch follow-through. Entertainment publicity and brand strategy retainers are scoped individually by career stage and services bundled in — confirmed on a first call.

Scope Beyond Media Relations

Some engagements need only press pitching. Others need the pitching to connect to a longer-term brand story.

East 2 West Collective: Our brand marketing work folds together brand strategy, media relations, public relations, strategic partnership development, internal and crisis communications, influencer engagement, and experiential marketing, so a single announcement, launch, or crisis response reinforces every other piece of a client's public story.

Communication & Point of Contact

Know who you'll actually work with day to day, and how to reach them before a decision moment arrives.

FAQ

Should I get quotes from more than one PR firm before deciding? Yes. PR retainers vary by scope, minimum engagement length, and how much strategy work is bundled in, so a written proposal from more than one firm makes the comparison concrete instead of based on reputation alone.

What should a PR firm's proposal or first call actually cover? It should cover the retainer range, the minimum engagement length, what's included in onboarding (media list build, positioning work), and what triggers a different price within that range — career stage, media list scope, or bundled services like brand strategy or crisis communications.

Does a bigger-name PR firm always mean better results? Not necessarily. A firm's fit depends on whether its specialization, market presence, and case studies match the specific campaign — an entertainment or book launch campaign benefits from a firm that runs that work regularly and can show named placements.

How long is a typical PR engagement? It depends on the type of campaign. East 2 West Collective's book launch campaigns run a 3-month minimum, built around a publication date arc rather than sold in shorter increments.

What's the difference between media relations and full brand strategy? Media relations is press pitching and outreach. Brand strategy adds positioning, messaging, strategic partnerships, and crisis communications underneath that outreach, so press coverage builds toward a longer-term story rather than standing alone.

Is East 2 West Collective a fit for both authors and entertainment talent? Yes. East 2 West Collective runs entertainment publicity and book launch campaigns from the same Los Angeles and New York offices, including campaigns for public figures who are also authors.

Both East 2 West Collective and 42West are worth a written quote before you decide — compare the scope, price range, and proof of results each firm puts in writing, not just the conversation on a first call.
