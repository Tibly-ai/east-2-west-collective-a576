Who you'll work with

01

Elizabeth Much

Founder

emuch@e2wcollective.com

Elizabeth Much is the CEO of E2W Collective Public Relations, where she works with a variety of clients from best-selling authors to brands and award-winning actors, filmmakers, TV stars and comedians.

Her day-to-day covers strategy, media relations across broadcast, digital and print, crisis counsel and media training for clients heading into their first national interview.

02

Sydney Small

Publicity & Client Services

ssmall@e2wcollective.com

Sydney keeps campaigns moving on the timeline set at kickoff: interviews with the client, the media blueprint, query letters and biographies, photo shoots, then the pitching itself. Day-to-day pitching runs across broadcast bookers, digital editors and print desks, plus the follow-through that turns one booking into the next.

She also handles the logistics clients feel most on event days: media outreach before and after, red carpet arrivals, interview and photo set-ups.

What the team covers

Every desk, in-house

The same people who plan the campaign write the materials and make the calls. Here is the range of work that runs through the office.

Media Relations

Broadcast, digital and print pitching, plus press release development and distribution to the outlets that actually reach your audience.

Entertainment Publicity

Established and emerging talent, television networks and properties, individual shows and feature films.

Brand Strategy

Brand marketing consultancy, positioning and messaging for brands and individuals with a service to sell.

Crisis & Media Training

Crisis management, internal and crisis communications, and media training before the camera turns on.

Partnerships & Influencers

Strategic partnership development, influencer engagement and experiential marketing that gives people something to talk about.

Events

Media outreach before and after, corporate sponsor conversations, celebrity attendance, red carpet coordination and on-site interviews.

Book Launches

Campaigns for authors and publishers, including New York Times best-selling titles: target audience strategy, national broadcast bookings, excerpt and roundup placements, virtual events and launch-week timing.
