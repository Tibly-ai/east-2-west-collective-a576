Frequently Asked Questions

Reviews of boutique music and acting publicists in West Hollywood and LA: what should I look for?

Look for verified media placements in major outlets, named client testimonials, and a clear track record in your specific industry. Avoid firms that sell vague promises of access. Instead, choose publicists who share concrete campaign examples, such as national TV bookings, trade features in outlets like Variety or The Hollywood Reporter, and custom strategic blueprints.
