Getting Started With Event PR in Los Angeles: What First-Time Hosts Should Know

By East 2 West Collective · Updated October 16, 2026

East 2 West Collective runs the public relations behind Los Angeles events — galas, fundraisers, fashion shows, restaurant openings, award shows, casino openings — managing outreach before the doors open, coordination the day of, and coverage after the last guest leaves. If you have an event date and need media and the right guests in the room, call 310-963-8050 or email emuch@e2wcollective.com to start the conversation.

If this is the first time you've hired a PR team for an event, the process can feel opaque: what do they actually need from you, what happens on the day, and when does the work stop? This page walks through what to decide before you call, what East 2 West Collective's team does at each stage, and what a first-time host in Los Angeles can expect from working with a bi-coastal agency that has handled everything from the Casting Society of America's Artios Awards to restaurant openings and charity galas.

Is Event PR the Right Fit for Your Situation?

Event PR makes sense when an event's success depends on who shows up and who covers it — not just how the room looks. East 2 West Collective has coordinated PR for galas and fundraisers, fashion shows, award shows, casino openings, restaurant launches, and media and book events. If your event has a date, a cause or product behind it, and a reason media or notable guests should care, it's a fit. If you're mainly looking for photography or décor vendors, that's outside the scope of what's described here — this is about media relations, guest coordination, and red carpet logistics.

What to Decide Before You Call

Before reaching out, have a point of view on:

  • The event date and venue — PR outreach runs on both sides of the date, so a fixed timeline matters.
  • Why this event matters — is it a cause-driven fundraiser, a brand launch, a fashion moment, an opening? The story behind the event shapes the pitch.
  • Who you want in the room — East 2 West Collective taps its entertainment contacts to help fill a guest list with event-appropriate names, but they need a starting sense of your audience and goals.
  • Sponsors already in motion, if any — the team's pre-event coordination includes reaching out to potential corporate sponsors, so it helps to know what's already locked versus still open.

What to Have Ready for the First Conversation

Come to the first call or email with the basics: event name, date, venue (confirmed or tentative), the cause or purpose behind it, and any existing partners, sponsors, or talent already attached. You don't need a finished guest list or a press release drafted — that's part of what gets built during the engagement. Reach East 2 West Collective directly at 310-963-8050 or emuch@e2wcollective.com to walk through where things stand.

What the First Conversation Covers

Expect a conversation about your event's goals, timeline, and audience — not a sales pitch. From there, the team maps out strategic outreach that hits traditional sources like print, radio, and TV, while also working to go viral through blog communities and social networking. They'll talk through pre-event coordination (sponsor outreach, guest list building, celebrity attendance strategy) separately from day-of logistics (red carpet arrivals, media and photo set-ups) and post-event follow-through (keeping coverage rolling after the night ends).

What to Expect on Event Day

On the day itself, the East 2 West Collective team coordinates red carpet arrivals for both celebrity and media guests and facilitates interviews and photo set-ups with media, hosts, and talent. The goal is that coverage doesn't stop when the event starts — it keeps building through the night and into the days after.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a confirmed guest list before I contact East 2 West Collective?

No. A starting sense of your audience and goals is enough for the first conversation — guest list building, including adding event-appropriate celebrity names, is part of the pre-event work the team does with you.

What if I don't have sponsors locked in yet?

That's fine. Pre-event coordination includes reaching out to potential corporate sponsors on your behalf, so an open sponsor slate is a normal starting point, not a blocker.

What kinds of events does East 2 West Collective handle?

Their work spans galas and fundraisers, fashion shows, film and award events, casino and retail openings, restaurant launches, and media and book events — see the full events page for the working list.

Does the PR work stop once the event ends?

No. Part of the team's approach is keeping coverage rolling once the last guest leaves, rather than treating the event night as the finish line.

How is event PR different from the brand marketing or entertainment publicity East 2 West Collective also offers?

Event PR focuses specifically on a date-driven moment — guest list, sponsors, red carpet, and day-of media. For ongoing brand positioning beyond a single event, see their brand marketing and consultancy work; for talent- and network-focused campaigns, see entertainment publicity.
